Spell Slots

Slots track how many casts you have available at each slot level. The source assigned to a spell supplies its casting stats; it does not force that spell to use a particular slot pool.

For shared multiclass spellcasting, enter the combined slot totals once under one source. Keep Pact Magic in a separate pool, usually with Short Rest recovery. This panel edits the recorded pools; it does not enforce which pool a spell can use.
